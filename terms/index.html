These terms apply to every member, dependent and guest of Meenaa's Millionaire Club® and to everyone who uses this website. They sit alongside the Club's bye-laws and the rules displayed at each club house.

01Admission & eligibility

  1. 1.1

    Membership is by invitation and referral. Every application must be proposed and seconded by existing members or by persons approved by the Governing Body.

  2. 1.2

    The Governing Body (“the Committee”) has absolute discretion to accept, defer or decline any application, without being obliged to give reasons.

  3. 1.3

    Primary members must be at least 21 years of age. Applicants may be asked for identity, address, professional, service or company documents relevant to their category.

  4. 1.4

    Categories marked “subject to eligibility” or “subject to service verification” are confirmed only after the documents are verified.

  5. 1.5

    Membership is personal. It may not be lent, sold, sub-let or used by anyone other than the member and the dependents registered with the Club.

02Membership contributions

  1. 2.1

    Contributions shown on this website are one-time admission contributions in Indian Rupees. GST and any other applicable taxes are charged in addition.

  2. 2.2

    Admission contributions are non-refundable once membership is approved, except where the Club itself is unable to offer the category applied for.

  3. 2.3

    Membership gives access to club facilities and services only. It is not an investment, deposit, share or security, and carries no promise of returns, interest or resale value.

  4. 2.4

    Annual subscriptions, facility charges, staff welfare contributions and other recurring charges, where applicable, are billed separately as notified by the Club.

  5. 2.5

    The Club may revise contributions and charges for new applicants at any time. Revisions do not affect contributions already paid.

  6. 2.6

    Payments are accepted only through the Club’s official accounts after written confirmation from the Secretary. The Club is not responsible for payments made in any other way.

03Branches & reciprocal access

  1. 3.1

    Branch access depends on the membership category: All-India (19 places), South (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai — 7 places), West (Mumbai, Ahmedabad — 6 places) or North & East (Delhi, Kolkata — 6 places), or as stated against the category.

  2. 3.2

    Places open in phases. Facilities differ between places and are finalised for each location.

  3. 3.3

    Use of guest rooms, private dining, halls and visits to places outside a member’s own circuit require advance booking and are subject to availability.

  4. 3.4

    Reciprocal arrangements with other clubs, where offered, are governed by the rules of the host club and may be limited in number of days per year.

04Dependents & family

  1. 4.1

    A member’s spouse and children may be registered as dependents and use the Club with the member’s card.

  2. 4.2

    A dependent child’s access ends at 21 years of age unless the child is admitted in their own right under an applicable category.

  3. 4.3

    Children under 16 must be accompanied by a responsible adult in all areas, and may use bars, lounges and certain facilities only as notified.

05Guests

  1. 5.1

    Each member may introduce up to 3 guests per visit, unless the Club notifies otherwise for an event or facility.

  2. 5.2

    Guests must be signed in at reception and must remain in the company of their host member while on Club premises.

  3. 5.3

    The host member is responsible for the conduct of their guests and is liable for all charges and any damage they cause.

  4. 5.4

    The Club may limit how often the same guest is introduced and may decline entry to any guest.

06Dress code

  1. 6.1

    Smart casual dress is expected in all areas during the day.

  2. 6.2

    After 8 PM in the main dining rooms and lounges: suit, blazer or formal Indian wear for gentlemen, and formal or traditional attire for ladies.

  3. 6.3

    Sportswear is permitted only in sports and fitness areas. Shorts, vests and open slippers are not permitted in dining rooms, lounges or the reception.

07Conduct & privacy

  1. 7.1

    Members and guests are expected to behave with courtesy towards fellow members and staff at all times.

  2. 7.2

    Photographs or videos of other members and guests may not be taken or shared without their consent. Some areas may be marked as no-photography areas.

  3. 7.3

    Mobile phones must be on silent in dining rooms, lounges and libraries. Calls may be taken in designated areas only.

  4. 7.4

    Commercial soliciting of fellow members (selling, pitching or canvassing for business) is not permitted except at Club networking events organised for that purpose.

  5. 7.5

    Harassment, discrimination, intoxicated or disorderly behaviour, gambling and the carrying of weapons are strictly prohibited.

  6. 7.6

    Music and noise on outdoor lawns are limited after 10 PM. Personal speakers are not permitted in common areas.

08Billing & dues

  1. 8.1

    The Club operates cashless. Charges are settled by member card, UPI, debit or credit card, or billed to the member’s account.

  2. 8.2

    Direct tipping of staff is not permitted. Staff welfare is supported through the Club, as notified to members.

  3. 8.3

    Monthly statements are payable within 30 days. Dues unpaid after 30 days attract interest at 18% per annum, and members may have their privileges suspended until the account is cleared.

  4. 8.4

    Members remain liable for all charges incurred by themselves, their dependents and their guests.

09Bookings & events

  1. 9.1

    Private halls, banquets and event spaces require an advance deposit at the time of booking.

  2. 9.2

    Cancellations made less than 72 hours before the booking may be charged in full, including any minimum spend.

  3. 9.3

    Invitations to Club events are personal and non-transferable unless the invitation says otherwise.

10Transfer & nomination

  1. 10.1

    Lifetime membership may be transferred once, to a legal heir, with the approval of the Governing Body and payment of the applicable transfer fee.

  2. 10.2

    Corporate and institutional members may change their nominees up to twice a year, subject to approval and the applicable fee.

  3. 10.3

    Life Time Temporary Memberships (LTTM) are personal to the member, carry the tenure and voting rights stated for the category, and are not transferable.

11Discipline, suspension & termination

  1. 11.1

    Before any disciplinary action, the member will receive a written show-cause notice and at least 14 days to respond, and may be heard by the managing committee.

  2. 11.2

    The managing committee may warn, fine or suspend a member. Expulsion requires a two-thirds majority of the committee.

  3. 11.3

    A member may resign by writing to the Secretary. Contributions are not refunded on resignation, suspension or expulsion, and any dues remain payable.

12Changes to these terms

  1. 12.1

    The Club may amend these terms, its bye-laws and its rules from time to time. The version published on this website and the Club notice board applies from the date shown.

  2. 12.2

    Where these terms differ from the Club’s registered bye-laws, the bye-laws prevail.

13Disputes & jurisdiction

  1. 13.1

    Any dispute between a member and the Club will first be referred to the Governing Body for resolution.

  2. 13.2

    Disputes that are not resolved will be settled by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration is Bengaluru, and the courts at Bengaluru have exclusive jurisdiction.

14Privacy

  1. 14.1

    This website does not store the details you type. The enquiry and newsletter forms open your own email app, addressed to the Club Secretary.

  2. 14.2

    Details you share with the Club are used only to process your enquiry or membership and to keep you informed about the Club. They are never sold.

  3. 14.3

    You may ask the Secretary at any time to see, correct or delete the personal details the Club holds about you.

  4. 14.4

    The map on the Contact page is provided by Google and is subject to Google’s own privacy policy.

15Website & content

  1. 15.1

    Information on this website is indicative and may change. Photography is illustrative unless stated otherwise.

  2. 15.2

    The Meenaa’s Millionaire Club® name and logo may not be used without written permission.
